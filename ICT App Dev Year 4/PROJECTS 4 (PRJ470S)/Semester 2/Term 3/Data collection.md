Summary You Can Use in Your Research Paper (Data Collection, Analysis & Findings Chapter)
(Based on your supervisor’s conversation)

1. Data Collection Approach
Your study uses secondary data, meaning you rely on existing published articles already in the public domain.

The correct method for this is a Systematic Literature Review (SLR).

SLR is a qualitative research method because it involves interpreting and synthesizing existing literature rather than generating numerical data.

2. Systematic Literature Review Requirements
You must review articles systematically, following a clear process:

Define inclusion/exclusion criteria

Identify themes

Extract relevant findings

Compare authors

Synthesize insights

Your supervisor emphasized that SLR = qualitative, not quantitative.

3. Data Analysis
After collecting articles, you must:

Identify themes emerging from the literature

Group findings according to your research questions

Interpret how authors address each theme

Link these findings back to your literature review

The analysis section should not repeat your research questions.

In the literature review, questions appear as questions.

In the findings chapter, they become themes or discussion headings.

4. Findings Section
Present findings as themes, not questions.

Each theme must:

Summarize what the literature says

Compare authors

Highlight patterns, contradictions, gaps

Use multiple sources (not over‑relying on one author)

Your supervisor stressed that you must back up each theme with literature, including the articles you uploaded (e.g., ART01 Sithole).

5. Discussion Section
This is where you interpret the findings.

You must:

Explain what the findings mean

Link them to your literature review

Show how they answer your research questions

This section is no longer a Q&A, but a narrative discussion.

6. Structure of the Chapter
Your chapter must follow this flow:

Introduction to the chapter

Data Collection Method (SLR)

Data Analysis Process

Findings (Themes)

Discussion of Findings

Chapter Summary

Conclusion & Recommendations

Confirm each research question was answered

Provide recommendations based on findings

7. Document Continuity
All sections remain in one document (your full research report).

You may draft separately, but final submission must be merged.
