2.1 Recommend process model for EduTrack? Recommended: Agile/Scrum. Justification: frequent requirement changes suit iterative delivery; each sprint produces working software; client can review and redirect after each sprint; tight 6-month timeline benefits from early partial releases. [3 marks recommendation+justification, 3 marks addressing change, 3 marks addressing timeline]
2.2 Scrum sprint planning for LMS? Sprint planning involves: Product Owner prioritising the product backlog; Development Team selecting items they can complete in the sprint; Team breaking items into tasks and estimating effort; Sprint Goal defined. Roles: Product Owner (priority), Scrum Master (facilitates), Development Team (commits to work). [2 marks per role explained, 3 marks for process, 1 mark sprint goal]
3.1 Use case: Transfer Money? Actor: Authenticated customer. Precondition: Logged in, source account has sufficient funds. Main flow: (1) Select Transfer option. (2) Choose source account. (3) Enter destination account number. (4) Enter amount. (5) Confirm transfer. (6) System validates and processes. (7) Confirmation message and reference number displayed. Alternate flow: Insufficient funds → error message. Postcondition: Both accounts updated; notification sent. [1.5 marks per step, up to 6 = 9 marks]
3.2 Two non-functional requirements for money transfer? (1) Security — all data must be encrypted (TLS 1.2+); two-factor authentication required to prevent unauthorised transactions. Critical because financial data is highly sensitive. (2) Performance — transfer must complete within 5 seconds under normal load. Critical because slow transactions reduce trust and usability. [3 marks each: 1 name, 1 explanation, 1 justification]
