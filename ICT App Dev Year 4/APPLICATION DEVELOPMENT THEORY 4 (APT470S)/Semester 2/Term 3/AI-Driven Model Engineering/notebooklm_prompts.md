Dominant AI Technologies & Generation MechanismsThe requirement-to-model space is dominated by Generative Large Language Models (LLMs), fine-tuned Transformer architectures (Seq2Seq / Seq2AST), and Hybrid Neuro-Symbolic Systems11more_horiz. By generating AST node sequences rather than raw text tokens, models like GenClass eliminate syntax errors and reduce parameter overhead (60M parameters vs. 100B+ LLMs)1121.Hybrid & Neuro-Symbolic Pipelines (e.g., VeriGen, MoRe, AGREE-Dog): Combine generative LLMs with deterministic validation loops13more_horiz.
