MARKING MEMORANDUM (INTERNAL USE)

SECTION A

* Q1: 1.1 b; 1.2 b; 1.3 c; 1.4 c; 1.5 b. (1 mark each)
* Q2: Internal Work Products: Work products consumed only by project participants (2 marks). Deliverables: Work products delivered to the customer (2 marks).
* Q3: List: Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring & Controlling, Closing (1 mark for the complete list). Description: 1 mark per correct brief description (5 marks). Total: 6 marks.

SECTION B

* Q4: Prevoyant: Forecast and plan strategy. Organise: Build material and human structure. Command: Maintain activity among personnel. Coordinate: Unify and harmonise effort. Control: Ensure conformity with established rules. (1 mark each)
* Q5: Unity of Command: One employee reports to one boss. Remuneration: Fair payment for services. Equity: Equality of treatment.
  * The Exam Trap (Rationale): Students often confuse financial payment (Remuneration) with fair/equal treatment (Equity). Remuneration is about the "paycheck" (not just what the company can get away with), while Equity is about fairness in the "treatment" of people. (2 marks per principle/distinction)
* Q6: In a Matrix Org, an employee reports to two people: the Department Head and the Project Manager. Fayol’s Unity of Command dictates that an employee should have one, and only one, boss. Reporting to two individuals creates conflict in command. (4 marks)

SECTION C

* Q7: Liaison (Interpersonal); Monitor (Informational); Entrepreneur (Decisional); Negotiator (Decisional). (1 mark each)
* Q8: Scenario A: Disturbance Handler. Rationale: The manager is reactive to an external pressure or accident outside their control. Scenario B: Entrepreneur. Rationale: The manager is proactive; they initiated a project after identifying an opportunity. (3 marks each)

SECTION D

* Q9:
  * Functional: 1 boss; Limited/Fixed resource flexibility.
  * Matrix: 2 bosses; Rapid response/Easy recruitment of specialised skills.
  * Project-Based: 1 boss (per project); Dynamic/Assigned for the duration. (1 mark per cell; 6 marks total)
* Q10: Projects pass through a "pipeline" between departments. The structure is fixed and permanent, meaning few participants are involved for the project’s full duration, leading to silos and potential delays in project-specific focus. (4 marks)
